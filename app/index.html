⚠ Only one trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. The first is Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after Down to Earth: find Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. That spot disappears after Chapter 1, so do it before the chapter ends and save manually right after.

Road to the Platinum — roughly 200 hours, difficulty 4/10

A single playthrough, no mission replay, no difficulty settings, no online trophies. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 save slots). Cheats disable trophies and saving while active.

Step 1 — The story 60–80 hours

Play Chapters 1–5 and finish the mission trophies and side-character finales as they come. Scan vehicles and study animals with the WAiNK app and binoculars from the start — 200 vehicles and 100 animals are needed later. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1.

Step 2 — 100% completion about 100 hours

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The checklist is lenient — about 60% of true completion: 100 optional tasks, 39 Gang Operations, 22 Bank Jobs, 8 bank vaults, 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, 300 stunts, 70 of 80 Challenges and more. Nothing on it is missable.

Step 3 — Cleanup 10–20 hours

Finish the Stranger strands, the last side activities, the spending and property trophies, then check the list: once A Long Road pops, Vice Got a Price! follows immediately.
